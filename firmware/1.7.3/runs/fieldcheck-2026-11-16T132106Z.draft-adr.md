**Status:** Proposed

## Context

FieldCheck 1.0 ran level 3 on an OP-1 with firmware 1.7.3 (tested), on iPad with iPadOS 27.2.1, starting 2026-11-16T13:21:06Z. It compared the run with the baseline for firmware 1.7.3, level 3, from the run started 2026-11-15T16:17:28Z.

Results: 3 pass, 0 changed, 1 fail, 2 not run. Each change and failure follows, with what FieldCheck suggests. The suggestions are for a person to confirm, and FieldCheck makes no recommendation.

### `reconnect.kept`: failed

- **Expected:** After leaving MTP mode, a written tape and its tape.json are kept, apart from pos.
- **Used by:** Restore
- **Simulator:** the simulated OP-1 and its tests that name `reconnect.kept`.
- **Seen:** The OP-1 disconnected during this check.
- **Suggested class, to confirm:** Incompatible. The behavior FieldTapeKit relies on is gone on this firmware.
